Choosing DDP or DDU for Each Shipment

LogisticsImports2026
What I Did

Compared DDP forwarder rates with DDU shipping through Amazon Global Logistics, including duties and the impact on landed cost and COGS.

Switched low-duty items to DDU through AGL to lower freight costs, with a US LLC set up as importer of record. Items where DDP works out cheaper stay on DDP, and I check the rates for each shipment before choosing.

Result

Lower freight costs on low-duty items, with both options kept open so every shipment goes the cheaper way.
